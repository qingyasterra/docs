Understanding results

Measurements are observations made at a particular time, from your current connection, using the selected method and endpoint. Network congestion, device conditions, server behavior, filtering, and permissions can affect results.

  • Speed test results measure transfers to a test endpoint and may differ from your provider's advertised speed or the performance of other services.
  • Response-time checks may measure HTTPS requests rather than ICMP echo responses. Timeouts do not by themselves establish that a device is offline or identify the cause of a fault.
  • Regional checks measure reachability to selected regional endpoints from your connection; they do not measure all networks in a region or run tests from that region.
  • Route tracing and MTU probing use IPv4. MTU results describe tested packet sizes and confirmed responses, not a guarantee of the exact path MTU.
  • Public network checks, certificate details, and device trust labels do not certify that a network, website, or device is secure.
  • Room samples reflect connection performance at the time of sampling; they are not a calibrated radio signal survey. Manually entered data budgets may differ from your carrier's billing records.

Use the results as diagnostic information and verify findings before making consequential changes. The Service does not promise uninterrupted operation, a particular connection speed, or a successful diagnosis or repair.
